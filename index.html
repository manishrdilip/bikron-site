FAQ

Questions shops ask

Do you need access to our files or network?

No network access needed. We build and test on sample files you choose to share, under an NDA if you prefer. The finished tools run on your own computers, so your data stays with you.

Which SOLIDWORKS versions and ERPs do you support?

SOLIDWORKS 2020 and newer. For ERP we produce import files (CSV or Excel) that work with most systems. We confirm compatibility with yours during the free audit.

Does anything go into our ERP without a person checking it?

No. The tool prepares and validates the import file and flags problems; an engineer approves it before it goes in.

What if automation isn't worth it for us?

Then the audit says so. You get the one-page report either way, free.

How do we pay?

By invoice: Interac e-Transfer or credit card. 50% to start, balance on delivery.
