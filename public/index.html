FAQ

Questions manufacturers ask

Do you need access to our network or ERP?

No. We build and test on sample files you choose to share, under an NDA if you prefer. The finished tools run on your own computers, so your data stays with you.

Which SOLIDWORKS versions and ERPs do you support?

SOLIDWORKS 2020 and newer. For ERP we produce validated import files (CSV or Excel) that work with most systems. We confirm compatibility with yours during the free audit.

Does anything go into our ERP without a person checking it?

No. Tools prepare and validate the data and flag problems. A person on your team approves before anything is imported.

What happens if you miss the delivery date?

You get 10% off the setup fee. The date is written into your proposal.

We're outside Ontario, or outside Canada. Can you help?

Yes. All work is delivered remotely, with calls scheduled in your time zone. Pricing is available in CAD or USD.

What if automation isn't worth it for us?

Then the audit says so, and you keep the one-page report either way, free.

How do we pay?

By invoice: Interac e-Transfer, wire or credit card. 50% to start, balance on delivery. Care Plan billed monthly.
